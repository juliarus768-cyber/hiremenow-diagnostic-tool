Employer decision clarity

Why Positioning Matters

Qualified professionals can be missed when their value is not immediately clear.

Employers make decisions quickly, often with limited context. A strong resume does more than summarize experience; it helps the reader understand the level you operate at, the value you provide, where you fit, and why you should be interviewed.

The Career Positioning Diagnostic brings a calm, consultant-led lens to these questions so your resume can be evaluated against the opportunity you are actually pursuing.

  • What level you operate at
  • What value you provide
  • Where you fit
  • Why you should be interviewed
